Warp Core

thousands of operations and conditions, one GPU

A Godot game's rules and physics, run as GPU kernels across thousands of independent copies at once. Godot becomes the window you watch one copy through.

What you get
Bulk simulation of your Godot scene, with bug findings from thousands of parallel runs, parity gates against Godot and fault traces to the variable where each began.
Measured
5.05M world-steps/sec at 4,096 operations and conditions; 10 of 10 injected bug classes traced. Simple test scene.
Engage
Design-partner pilot, scoped per studio.
