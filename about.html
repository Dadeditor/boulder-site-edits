Boulder Research / About

The company,
plainly.

Boulder Research is a small company building software for an operation and its conditions: AI that predicts how they respond to action, grounded in a real simulation and efficient enough to run on a single GPU. Three people, two cities, one product in pilots.

02 / The link mapPerson · company · product

Three sites,
three different things.

Chris Kellogg builds and runs a small portfolio of connected work. Boulder Research, founded with Adhitya Mohan and Akhil S, is the company: it researches how an operation and its conditions respond, on a single GPU, starting with Warp Core, which runs thousands of copies of a Godot game in parallel, checks them against the engine and traces faults to their cause. Miniloader is the product: a local-AI platform for running agents and models on your own hardware. Chris is the person behind both, covering product, rapid prototyping and AI infrastructure, and turning research into shipped software.

boulderresearch.ai

The company. This site: the company story, the projects and the research that feeds them, and the investor materials.

kellogg.io

The person. Chris Kellogg's personal site. One of the founders, separate from the company and its work.

miniloader.ai

Miniloader, a research project. Miniloader is a visual control panel for running agents, models and tools on your own hardware — a research project of Boulder Research that ships. It runs on its own domain; see its project page. Its hosted sibling, Astrolabe (astrolabe.miniloader.ai), is an adjacent product, not a research project.

03 / Site mapWhere everything lives

How the site
is organised.

Home

index.html — the company story, from the thesis to the team. Served at the root of the domain.

Projects

projects.html — the menu item for everything that ships: Warp Core, Miniloader and Astrolabe. "Projects" in the top menu opens a dropdown into these pages.

Research

research.html — the technical essays, grouped by topic (operations and conditions, simulation architecture, verification, hardware, method), not a flat list. It is not a top-level menu item: Research sits inside the Projects dropdown, because the essays feed the projects rather than standing on their own.

Investors

investors.html — the fundraising track. The drafts brief.html and warp-core-brief.html live here, not in the top menu, so they are never mistaken for research.

For reference

The design studies archive/homepage-concepts.html (five front-page concepts) and archive/what-happens-next.html (an earlier front-page design) are kept in the repo's archive/ directory for reference. They are not published and are not part of the menu, so they are named here rather than linked.
